Use Case 2:

Business Need
We have developed a predictive FDTL dashboard that proactively identifies flight duties likely to breach DGCA CAR limits, enabling early intervention by crew controllers. 
However, the choice of corrective action remains manual and experience driven, leading to inconsistent decisions and increased cognitive load in time critical operational scenarios.

Proposed Solution
Build an AI driven recommendation engine that learns from historical FDTL breaches, duty characteristics, and actions taken, to intelligently suggest optimal corrective actions for crew controllers in real time.

Use Case Description
For each predicted FDTL breach, the AI model will
•	Analyze the nature of the duty (sector count, reporting time, night stop, fatigue risk, aircraft type, crew type, etc.)
•	Learn from historical actions taken in similar situations and their outcomes
•	Consider operational constraints at that point in time (standby availability, network impact, extension limits)
•	Recommend possible actions such as duty split, pilot consent with extension, standby activation, duty break, or flight cancellation
•	Provide ranked recommendations with brief reasoning to support decision making

Business Value
•	Reduces dependency on individual judgment by providing data driven, consistent recommendations
•	Improves speed and quality of decision making during high pressure operational windows
•	Enhances regulatory compliance by proactively managing FDTL risks
•	Optimizes crew utilization while minimizing operational disruption
•	Acts as a decision support tool, not automation, keeping final control with crew controllers

Strategic Outcome
Transforms the FDTL dashboard from a predictive monitoring tool into an intelligent decision support system, enabling smarter, faster, and more consistent crew operations management.
