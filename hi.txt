Brief story
The film unfolds quietly, following a young woman through the ritual of flying home after a long separation. Tension builds in the stillness of waiting, the intimacy of the plane cabin, the melancholy sight of the city receding, and the vulnerable walk into arrivals. The visual motif is the glow of her phone screen catching her face—a lifeline to home. The break occurs when she finally turns the phone off before reuniting. The piece is composed with slow tracking shots and long takes, ending on a held embrace that lingers beyond the cut.
