URTI Update Process Overview

When a crew member suffers from Upper Respiratory Tract Infection (URTI), the following steps are followed to update their leave status:
1. Initial Sick Marking
•	The crew is first marked sick by the Crew Tracking team based on base-side inputs.
•	The crew typically has 12 Sick Leave (SL) and 6 URTI leave balances. 

2. Base Confirmation
•	The base team confirms: 
o	IGA ID
o	Crew Name
o	Start and End Dates for URTI leave.

3. CLMS Verification
•	The CLMS Portal is checked to: 
o	Confirm URTI leave balance.
o	Review the crew’s roster to ensure the dates provided align with sick markings or duty performed.

4. Roster & Leave Update
•	Once verified: 
o	The crew’s roster is updated with the URTI code.
o	Sick leave previously marked is pulled back from CLMS and credited back to the crew’s SL balance.
o	The same dates are then updated with the URTI code.

5. Bucket-Based Leave Management
•	Leave balances are divided across calendar buckets.
•	If the URTI dates fall in a previous bucket, the system checks the URTI balance in that bucket.
•	The leave is updated; accordingly, adjustment reflects in the current bucket & previous bucket as well. (deduction made in current bucket needs to be reversed).

6. Special Cases
•	If the crew was marked LWP (Leave Without Pay) due to lack of sick balance, and later URTI is approved: 
o	The leave is not considered unpaid.
o	The crew becomes eligible for arrears and efficiency bonus, which must be shared with the Payroll team.

________________________________________
📋 Standard Documentation Required
•	Medical Certificate from an ENT specialist confirming URTI diagnosis.
•	Fitness Certificate from ENT on the last day of sick leave.
•	URTI Leave Format (IGA, Name, From, To, Number of Days).
•	Follow-up on CLMS after 7 working days to confirm update
