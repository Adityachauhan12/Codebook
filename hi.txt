**Yes, but with important caveats.**

What you're describing is essentially a decision-support system that would monitor L4 alerts in real-time and recommend specific crew tracking actions. Here's what's realistic and what isn't:

The staging data you have is essentially a time-series of duty snapshots with FDP predictions updating every 15–30 minutes. For each category, the appropriate intervention is different. An FDP-driven L4 might need route restructuring (dropping a leg, adding a tech stop), not a crew swap. A crew-incompleteness L4 needs a staffing solution. A night-ops L4 might need schedule retiming. Your data already contains the features needed for this classification: FDP Variance, Night Landings Variance, Night FDP Variance, OPS_LEGS, crew completeness (CP/FO null checks), and criticality score.

Second, the model could calculate a "swap effectiveness score" — before recommending a crew change, it could check whether the incoming crew member's accumulated duty/rest history would actually change the Allowable FDP or Predicted FDP. Third, it could prioritize by time-to-duty. The data shows crew changes happening 4–18 hours before duty start. **What a model CANNOT do from this data alone:**

The staging files don't contain crew rest history, cumulative duty hours, or FDTL limits for individual crew members.
