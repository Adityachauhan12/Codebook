Brief story
This film explores the emotional levity of effortless travel. The grandmother’s journey is framed as serene and buoyant from the boarding gate to the reunion’s anticipation. The camera floats with her, emphasizing ease and gentle wonder. Silence and warm cabin light accompany her as she practises words for a new life. The film ends on her face aglow with sunrise—weightless, as if still in the sky.
