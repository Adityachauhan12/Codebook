## IndiGo Brand Guardrails — Image Prompt Writing Guide

These rules govern how image descriptions must be written for IndiGo campaigns.
Creative Studio enforces these server-side, so prompts must align with them — not repeat them.
Focus purely on the campaign concept, offer, and visual idea.

### Brand foundation
- Effortless, premium-feeling affordability
- Clean, precise, modern design
- Clutter-free communication
- Realistic and authentic imagery (not artistic or stylised unless requested)

### Colour
- Blue must dominate; secondary colours soft/pastel
- Never suggest red or purple dominant palettes

### Visual style
- Conceptual and insight-driven — not generic or decorative
- Evocative, atmospheric, destination-led storytelling
- No stock photography look, no posed/smiling stock faces
- No generic aviation imagery (aircraft shots, runways, airports)
- Faces allowed only if candid and natural

### Typography (for reference, enforced server-side)
- Bauhaus std only; clean, legible, minimal

### What to include in the image_description
- The campaign concept and emotional idea
- A specific destination, place, or single telling object
- Mood, atmosphere, light, and time of day
- Composition: where the subject sits, and where empty space is left

### What NOT to include in the image_description
- Brand rules (already injected server-side)
- Brand or airline names — describe the picture, not the advertiser
- The offer text itself, or any words to be rendered inside the image
- Channel or format labels ("email banner", "social post") — the aspect ratio
  is sent as a separate API parameter, so naming it here wastes prompt weight
- Logo placement instructions
- Font names
- Colour hex codes or Pantone references

### Example of a good prompt
"Kerala backwaters at dusk, a single houseboat drifting between palm-lined banks.
Still water, low warm light, unhurried. Boat small and low in frame, wide open
sky across the upper half."

### Example of a bad prompt
"IndiGo summer sale banner. Offer: fares from ₹1,499. Use IndiGo blue #1A1F71.
Place logo top-right. Bauhaus std font. 16:9 digital banner."
