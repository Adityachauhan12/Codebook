A 30-second IndiGo brand film about how effortless flying feels. A
woman in her sixties is flying to meet her new grandchild for the
first time, and she is delighted the whole way. Inside the IndiGo
cabin: a crew member in a deep indigo navy uniform helps her stow her
bag, she lifts the window shade onto bright morning cloud, she quietly
practises what she will say, and she lands beaming. Warm, light and
unhurried. Nothing goes wrong, nobody is anxious, and there are no
tears — the joy is in the ordinary details.
