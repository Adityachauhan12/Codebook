GROOMING ASSESSMENT TOOL - DEMO DISCLAIMER

⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTICE
This is a demonstration tool powered by AI. Results are generated automatically and may contain inaccuracies. Always rely on trained human inspectors for final grooming compliance decisions.

---

KNOWN LIMITATIONS & ENVIRONMENTAL FACTORS

Lighting Conditions
Poor, dim, or colored lighting can affect accuracy of color detection (hair, makeup, nail polish). Bright backlighting may obscure details.

Camera Angle & Resolution
Extreme angles, blurry footage, or low resolution may prevent accurate assessment of nails, stockings, or badge positioning.

Video Quality
Compression artifacts, motion blur, or low frame rate may cause misidentification of hairstyle, scarf knots, or accessory compliance.

Partial Visibility
If only upper body is visible, stockings, lower portions of uniform, and certain accessories cannot be assessed. These are marked (NOT VISIBLE).

Makeup Variation
Makeup appearance varies significantly under different lighting. The tool may misclassify shades or blending under inconsistent conditions.

---

WHAT THE TOOL ASSESSES

• Hairstyle (style, neatness, color, integrity)
• Makeup (base, eyes, lips, overall finish)
• Nails (length, shape, color, finish)
• Accessories (rings, earrings, watch, prohibitions)
• Uniform (tunic, scarf, name badge, stockings)

---

WHAT THE TOOL DOES NOT DO

• Does not verify identity or authenticity of the person in the image/video.
• Does not assess behavior, conduct, or customer service compliance.
• Does not detect subtle or subjective violations that require expert human judgment.
• Cannot assess items not visible in the frame.

---

BEST PRACTICES FOR ACCURATE RESULTS

• Use well-lit environments with natural or neutral lighting
• Ensure full-body or at least upper-body visibility
• Provide clear, high-resolution images or videos (minimum 480p)
• Keep camera steady and avoid extreme angles
• Use consistent white balance
