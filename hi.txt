The mart structure itself — where each row is a pilot-sector combination — is well-suited for sequence modeling. You could frame breach prediction as a time-series problem where, after each sector completes, the model updates its estimate of whether the remaining duty will breach. The REMAINING_FDP_PRED column effectively does this already, but a learned model could incorporate delay trends, weather, and airport congestion patterns that the current rule-based system doesn't.
